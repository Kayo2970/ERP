LEADS ERP Operations Manual

Official User Guide • M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences • Production Ready

1. Platform Overview

The LEADS ERP is the central operations hub for the LEADS Next Gen Centre at MSRUAS. It automates event workflows, task execution, financial reimbursements, digital passes, and committee ratings in a single reactive portal.

🔒 Security by Design

All departmental records are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Unrelated operations never lock each other, and all changes synchronize across logged-in computers every 7 seconds automatically.

2. First-Time Account Activation

  1. Open your institutional email to locate the official invitation message containing your activation link.
  2. Click the link to open the activation screen.
  3. Set a strong password (minimum 8 characters).
  4. Confirm your name, division, and phone number, then click Complete Activation.
  5. Sign in via the Login page.

⚠️ Single-Use Activation Tokens

For security, activation links expire 48 hours after generation. If your link has expired, request a Department Head or Administrator to click Resend Activation Link from the Members Directory.

3. Installing the Mobile App (PWA)

Access the portal on your phone with zero app store downloads:

  • Apple iPhone / iPad: Open the portal in Safari, tap the Share button, and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Google Chrome): Open in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, and select Install App or Add to Home Screen.

4. Role Privileges & Access Hierarchy

Your access is governed by 7 clearly defined authority tiers:

Tier Role Title Authorized Actions
Tier 1 Super User Root platform administration, global audit logs, encrypted backups, persona switching, and emergency lockdown.
Tier 2 Centre Head Executive campus authority, final budget approvals, Level-2 reimbursement clearance, and VIP guest management.
Tier 3 Department / Event Head Event approvals, Level-1 reimbursement audits, committee task delegation, and performance reviewer.
Tier 4 Advisory Board / Faculty Strategic view access to analytics, committee ratings, and institutional reports.
Tier 5 Core Committee Leads Event setup, sub-committee assignments, pass studio generation, form builder, and task planning.
Tier 6 Training Associates / Members Personal task progress reporting, expense reimbursement claims, and digital visiting card generation.
Tier 7 Alumni / Guests Archive access, visiting card contact exchange, and public event passes.

Events Management

Events track every stage of institutional activity from initial proposal to post-event reporting.

  1. Click Events → + Create Event.
  2. Specify title, date range, campus venue, budget estimate, and target capacity.
  3. Add functional sub-committees (Stage, Logistics, Social Media, Hospitality) with designated student leads.
  4. Submit proposal. Once approved by a Tier 2/3 head, the event becomes Active.

Event Passes & Live Scanner Kiosk

Issue QR passes and manage gate security on event day:

  • Pass Studio: Customize badges with attendee names, categories (VIP, Speaker, Delegate), and seat assignments.
  • Automated Delivery: Send high-resolution PDF/image passes with embedded QR codes directly to attendee emails.
  • Live Check-in Scanner: Gate volunteers open Check-in Scanner on mobile or tablet to scan badges. The system provides instantaneous green/red verification to prevent pass duplication.

Apple Wallet & Google Wallet Passes

Event passes and member visiting cards can be added directly to mobile digital wallets:

  • Apple Wallet (.pkpass): iPhone users tap Add to Apple Wallet for lock screen notifications and geo-fenced gate alerts when approaching the MSRUAS campus.
  • Google Wallet: Android users tap Save to Google Wallet for cloud-synced digital badge storage.

Task Management, Delegation & Gantt Timeline

Keep your event committee organized and on schedule:

  • Committee Delegation: Assign a task to an entire sub-committee or to specific individuals.
  • Timeline View: Switch to Gantt Timeline to inspect critical path dependencies and milestone overlaps.
  • Automated Alerts: The platform automatically emails assignees 24 hours and 2 hours prior to task deadlines.

Performance Ratings & Evaluations

After an event concludes, evaluate performance across standard metrics (1-5 stars):

  • Timeliness & Reliability
  • Quality of Deliverables
  • Team Collaboration & Communication
  • Ratings feed into institutional member recognition transcripts.

Procurement Requisitions

Submit and review equipment or hardware purchase requests:

  1. Go to Procurement → + New Request.
  2. Provide item descriptions, vendor quotes, and budget justification.
  3. Department Heads clear standard requisitions; major purchases route to Centre Head for final clearance.

Financial Reimbursements (Dual-Gate Audit)

Ensure quick and audited reimbursement for approved out-of-pocket expenses:

Dual-Verification Pipeline

Claim Submitted ➔ Gate 1: Faculty Audit ➔ Gate 2: Centre Head Clearance ➔ Disbursement Completed.

Attach clear bill photos (max 10MB) and tag the related event. Once approved, the disbursed amount automatically debits the linked event budget.

Budgeting, P&L & Income Sources

Live financial accounting for the centre:

  • Allocations: Track semester-wise departmental budget ceilings.
  • Income Tracking: Monitor external sponsorships, ticket sales, and grant receipts.
  • Exports: Export full fiscal balance sheets to Excel/CSV or executive PDF.

Unified Approvals Inbox

The Approvals page unifies pending event proposals, procurement requests, reimbursement claims, and creative designs into one actionable list for decision-makers.

Members Directory & User Administration

Administer accounts, role promotions, and offboarding:

  • Add Member: Input name, email, division, and role to generate an activation link.
  • Role Hierarchy Changes: Promote student associates to Core Leads with one click.
  • Deactivation: Immediately terminates active sessions for departing members.

Design Portal & Automated OCR Spellcheck

Review event posters and social media banners before publishing:

  • AI Text Scanning: The built-in OCR scans uploaded images to catch misspelled guest names, inaccurate event dates, or formatting typos.
  • Faculty Sign-Off: Media advisors review and approve creatives with watermarking status tracking.

Dynamic Form Builder & Public Submissions

Build custom event sign-up and feedback forms:

  • Drag-and-drop form builder with custom public URL slugs (e.g., /forms/ai-symposium).
  • Instant high-resolution QR code generator for event posters.
  • Real-time response tracking with one-click export to Word (.docx) and CSV.

Digital Visiting Cards & 3D Keycard

Every member has a personalized digital card accessible at /card/[slug]:

  • 3D Keycard: Interactive WebGL card reacting to touch and device motion.
  • vCard Download: Instant contact save to smartphone address books.
  • Wallet Pass: Apple & Google Wallet integration.

Guest Directory & VIP Invitation Engine

Manage visiting dignitaries, speakers, and industry delegates:

  • Visiting Card OCR: Take a photo of a physical business card to auto-populate contact details.
  • Batch Invites: Dispatch personalized VIP invitations with embedded RSVP links.

Backup & Disaster Recovery

Super Users can create encrypted snapshots in the Backup module. The resulting archive packages all 28 collections and uploaded files into a master-key encrypted package for safe offline storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How quickly do changes appear on another device?

A: The system automatically syncs every 7 seconds. You can also click any navigation link to immediately pull the latest data.

Q: How do I reset my password?

A: Click Forgot Password on the login screen to receive a secure reset link, or ask an administrator in the Members Directory.

Q: What should I do if an image won't upload?

A: Ensure the file size is under 10MB and is a valid JPG, PNG, or PDF file.
